# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in JIDRA a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Accept feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the project and community

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report issues by opening a GitHub issue or contacting the maintainers directly.
